    How this is computed

    Energy per cell = Σchannels (Δ / σch)², σ = [13.46 K, 7.55 mm/d, 2.90, 2.43 m/s, 619 Pa, 20.49 kg/m²]. Near is within 2 cells of the box; far is everything else (mean per cell, log scale). Reach is the furthest cell (Chebyshev distance in 1° grid cells from the box) with energy above 10⁻³, also drawn as a dotted ring on the Δ map.
